## 🚀 Key Features

1. **Prominent Exam Countdown & Active Study Clock**:
   - Live header shows: `Day X of 140 | ⏳ Y Days Remaining for GATE 2027`.
   - **Page Visibility Foreground Clock**: Tracks your actual study seconds strictly when the website is actively in the foreground (automatically pauses when you switch tabs or minimize).
2. **1-Step-At-A-Time Distraction-Free Flow**:
   - Only ONE activity visible on screen at a time:
     - **🌅 Step 1: 5-Min Recall**: Yesterday's cleared topics, formula blitz, and 3-question active recall test.
     - **📺 Step 2: In-App Video & Notes**: Embedded YouTube lectures (Theory vs PYQ toggle) + KaTeX formulas.
     - **🧮 Step 3: Interactive PYQ Solver**: Official GATE 1-mark & 2-mark questions (MCQ/NAT with $\pm 5\%$ tolerance, step-by-step mathematical proofs, and 1-click mistake tagging).
     - **✅ Step 4: 10-Second Wrap-Up**: Auto-populates study time from the foreground timer and advances to the next topic.
3. **🖩 On-Screen TCS iON Virtual Scientific Calculator**:
   - Authentic GATE exam calculator simulator with Deg/Rad toggle, trigonometry, logarithms, powers, and memory keys.
4. **📚 20-Week Syllabus Hierarchy**:
   - Structured across Engineering Math, IN Sensors & Transducers, Circuits, Signals & Control, RA Kinematics, Dynamics, Robot Vision, and General Aptitude.
5. **📊 Progress & Mistakes Diary**:
   - Quarantined mistakes table (`[C]`, `[F]`, `[A]`, `[I]`, `[T]`, `[S]`) with re-test workflows, 1-click formula cheat-sheet export, and JSON progress backup.
